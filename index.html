Flying like a pro

  • The exit. You start with no airspeed. Fall for a few seconds, then pull gently on S to pressurise the suit and round out into flight.
  • Energy is everything. Dive to trade height for speed, flare to trade speed for height. A flare from 250 km/h can buy you a 40 m climb.
  • Glide ratio. Your suit glides 2.2–3.1 m forward per metre of height. Gates sit on a line you can make — if you sink below them, you can’t climb back.
  • Proximity. Fly close to rock, trees and water for combo points that multiply over time. Crash and you lose the unbanked combo.
  • Stall. Too much angle of attack and the suit stops flying: lift drops, a wing can drop. Push forward to recover.
  • Thin air. In the Himalaya the air is half as dense — you fly 40% faster, controls feel loose, the canopy lands hot.
  • Canopy. Deploy above 200 m. It takes ~3 s and ~60 m to open. Land into the wind (watch the windsock) and flare at 3–4 m.
